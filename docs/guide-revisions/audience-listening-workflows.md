# Audience Listening: responsible query-to-reporting workflow (review draft)

**Status:** Draft pending program and privacy review.

## Design a listening question

Start with a specific reporting uncertainty and ask what members of the public can usefully tell the newsroom from direct experience. Use open questions that do not imply an answer, conflate multiple issues, demand private information unnecessarily or promise editorial outcomes. The [public query tool](https://tools.wuwf.org/audience-listening) supports a limited set of recorded questions; keep the sequence short enough to answer thoughtfully.

Examples:
- **Less useful:** "Why has rising rent made the city unaffordable for everyone?"
- **More useful:** "How, if at all, have changes in housing costs affected decisions you or your household made this year?"

The first assumes the conclusion; the second leaves room for differing experiences.

## Publish and review

1. Draft the query and review its public wording, recording experience and any consent/privacy notices before publication.
2. Share it through channels capable of reaching the audiences whose experiences matter; document obvious gaps in who is likely to encounter it.
3. Review incoming submissions for relevance, safety, follow-up potential and the limits of the speaker's firsthand knowledge.
4. Where a response merits reporting, seek corroboration and direct follow-up; do not treat a compelling recording as verified fact.
5. Send relevant responses to Sourcework using the existing handoff, keeping the originating query and source attribution linked.

## Interpret findings

Responses gathered through an open public prompt are **not a representative poll**. Do not equate the number of responses with prevalence across the coverage area. Note who was reached, who was not, which experiences recur and which observations merit additional reporting. Protect sensitive personal details and honor the actual consent terms presented to respondents.

## Close the loop

Where appropriate, explain what questions the newsroom heard and how they shaped subsequent coverage; do not imply that every submission will be broadcast or receive an individual response.

**Review before publication:** Read the live editor-modified query guides first and preserve their edits. Verify actual consent language, retention settings, audience access and Sourcework handoff before offering definitive privacy or disposal instructions.
